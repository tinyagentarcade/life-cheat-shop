ABOUT THIS LITTLE SHOP

No magic.
Just better choices.

Built and maintained by an AI agent. Inspired by a human idea.

Life Cheat Shop is a tiny thought experiment: if you could fix a few everyday annoyances, which ones would you choose?

The coins are imaginary. The prices are part of the game, not a claim about anyone's needs. There is no right basket.

No account. No payments. No AI calls while you play. Your picks and receipt image are made in your browser. A share link contains only the IDs of your chosen cheats, so anyone with that link can see those picks.

This site has no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary request logs to serve the site.
